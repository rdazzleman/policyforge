# Acme Corp Media Handling & Disposal Standard

## 1. Purpose

This Standard establishes Acme Corp's requirements for the protection,
handling, transport, and disposal of digital and non-digital media
containing sensitive, confidential, or regulated information.

## 4. Policy

### 4.1 Media Protection Policy and Procedures

Acme Corp shall develop, document, and disseminate a media protection
policy at the organization, mission/business process, or system level, as
appropriate. The policy must be reviewed and updated on a
**continuous** basis and following defined triggering events.
[NIST MP-1 Low/Moderate/High | HIPAA 164.310(d)(1)]

### 4.2 Documentation Retention and Maintenance

All media-protection policies, procedures, and related action and
assessment records must be maintained in written form (which may be
electronic). IT Asset Management shall retain such documentation for
**6 years** from the date of its creation or the date it was last in
effect, whichever is later.
[HIPAA 164.316(b)(1) | HIPAA 164.316(b)(2)(i) | HIPAA 164.316(b)(2)(ii)]

### 4.3 Access Restriction for Digital and Non-Digital Media

Access to defined types of media must be restricted to authorized
individuals only. Requests to remove media from a facility are logged in
[Ticketing System] and approved by the media custodian. [NIST MP-2]

### 4.4 Media Sanitization Review

Sanitization records are reviewed
[Assignment: organization-defined frequency] by IT Asset Management.
[NIST MP-6]

## 6. Evidence and Records

IT Asset Management shall ensure that the following evidence is generated,
reviewed, and retained as proof that this Standard is being followed:

| Evidence Artifact           | Description                                                                                                                    | Retention                |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------ |
| Certificates of Destruction | Documentation confirming that media has been securely and permanently destroyed, including method, date, and responsible party | Retained per Section 4.2 |
| Media transport log         | Record of each removal, transport, and return of media outside the facility                                                    | Retained per Section 4.2 |
