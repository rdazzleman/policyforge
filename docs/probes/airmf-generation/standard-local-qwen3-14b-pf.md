# Acme Health Information Security Standard: AI Risk Management

## 1. Legal and Regulatory Compliance for AI

The organization must ensure that all legal and regulatory requirements involving AI are understood, managed, and documented in accordance with organizational policies and procedures. [NIST AI RMF GOVERN-1.1]

## 2. Integration of Trustworthy AI Characteristics

The organization shall integrate the characteristics of trustworthy AI into its policies, processes, procedures, and practices, ensuring alignment with applicable frameworks and standards. [NIST AI RMF GOVERN-1.2]

## 3. Risk Management Activity Determination

Processes and procedures must be established to determine the needed level of risk management activities based on the organization’s risk tolerance, as defined in its risk management framework. [NIST AI RMF GOVERN-1.3]

## 4. Transparent Risk Management Controls

The risk management process and its outcomes must be established through transparent policies, procedures, and other controls, with prioritization based on the organization’s risk priorities. [NIST AI RMF GOVERN-1.4]

## 5. Ongoing Monitoring and Review of Risk Management

Ongoing monitoring and periodic review of the risk management process and its outcomes must be planned, with organizational roles and responsibilities clearly defined, including the determination of [Review Frequency]. [NIST AI RMF GOVERN-1.5]

## 6. Inventory and Resource Allocation for AI Systems

Mechanisms must be implemented to inventory AI systems, with resources allocated according to the organization’s risk priorities and the criticality of AI systems to operations. [NIST AI RMF GOVERN-1.6]

## 7. Safe Decommissioning of AI Systems

Processes and procedures must be in place for decommissioning and phasing out AI systems safely, ensuring that such actions do not increase risks or decrease the organization’s trustworthiness. [NIST AI RMF GOVERN-1.7]
