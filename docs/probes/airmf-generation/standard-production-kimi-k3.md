# Acme Health — AI Governance and Risk Management Standard

## 1. Purpose

This Standard establishes the mandatory governance and risk management requirements for artificial intelligence (AI) systems at Acme Health. It applies to all security, IT, compliance, and clinical informatics staff responsible for implementing, operating, or auditing AI systems within the organization. Given Acme Health's obligations as a healthcare organization, these requirements ensure that AI systems are governed in a manner consistent with applicable legal and regulatory obligations and the organization's risk tolerance.

## 2. Scope

This Standard applies to all AI systems developed, procured, deployed, operated, or decommissioned by or on behalf of Acme Health, and to all personnel and organizational units involved in their lifecycle.

## 3. Requirements

### 3.1 Legal and Regulatory Compliance

Acme Health shall ensure that legal and regulatory requirements involving AI are understood, managed, and documented. [NIST AI RMF GOVERN-1.1]

Responsible personnel shall maintain documentation of applicable AI-related legal and regulatory obligations and shall demonstrate how those obligations are managed across the AI system lifecycle.

### 3.2 Trustworthy AI Characteristics

The characteristics of trustworthy AI shall be integrated into Acme Health's organizational policies, processes, procedures, and practices. [NIST AI RMF GOVERN-1.2]

### 3.3 Risk-Based Level of Risk Management Activities

Acme Health shall establish and maintain processes, procedures, and practices to determine the needed level of risk management activities based on the organization's risk tolerance. [NIST AI RMF GOVERN-1.3]

### 3.4 Transparent Risk Management Process

The AI risk management process and its outcomes shall be established through transparent policies, procedures, and other controls based on organizational risk priorities. [NIST AI RMF GOVERN-1.4]

### 3.5 Ongoing Monitoring and Periodic Review

Acme Health shall plan for ongoing monitoring and periodic review of the AI risk management process and its outcomes. [NIST AI RMF GOVERN-1.5]

- Organizational roles and responsibilities for monitoring and review shall be clearly defined and documented. [NIST AI RMF GOVERN-1.5]
- The frequency of periodic review shall be determined and documented: [Review Frequency]. [NIST AI RMF GOVERN-1.5]

### 3.6 AI System Inventory

Acme Health shall establish and maintain mechanisms to inventory AI systems. [NIST AI RMF GOVERN-1.6]

- The inventory mechanism shall be resourced according to organizational risk priorities. [NIST AI RMF GOVERN-1.6]
- The inventory shall be maintained in [Asset Inventory System] and shall cover all AI systems within the scope of this Standard.

### 3.7 Decommissioning and Phase-Out

Acme Health shall establish and maintain processes and procedures for decommissioning and phasing out AI systems safely and in a manner that does not increase risks or decrease the organization's trustworthiness. [NIST AI RMF GOVERN-1.7]

## 4. Roles and Responsibilities

| Role                  | Responsibility                                                                                         |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| [AI Governance Owner] | Overall accountability for this Standard, including risk tolerance determinations and review frequency |
| Security/IT Staff     | Implementation of the processes, procedures, and controls required by this Standard                    |
| Compliance Staff      | Documentation and management of AI-related legal and regulatory requirements                           |
| Internal Audit        | Verification of conformance with this Standard                                                         |

## 5. Compliance and Enforcement

Conformance with this Standard is mandatory. Exceptions require documented approval by [AI Governance Owner] and must be recorded in [Ticketing System] with an associated risk acceptance.
